About the Project

The challenge. "Abandoned but Not Forgotten" is a NASA Space Apps challenge about telling the stories of NASA equipment, devices and spacecraft left behind on the Moon or Mars.

Why it is interesting. Every object has a story: why it was sent, what it achieved, and where it is today. Scientific details will be added after NASA data collection.

What we aim to do. We want to present these stories clearly, give a source for every fact, and let visitors check what they learned with a short quiz.

A student project for NASA Space Apps 2026. This is not an official NASA website.
