# Abandoned but Not Forgotten 🚀

**A NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 project by Team NEBIX**

[Visit the live website](https://mah-sar.github.io/abandoned-but-not-forgotten-NasaSpaceApps/)

## About the Project

Throughout space exploration, many missions have left equipment behind on the Moon and Mars. Although these machines are no longer operating, they remain important parts of human space history.

**Abandoned but Not Forgotten** is an interactive website that brings the stories of these forgotten pieces of space equipment back to life.

Our goal is to make space history easier, more interesting, and more accessible to everyone.

## What You Can Explore

The website features five pieces of space equipment:

1. **Apollo 11 Lunar Module Descent Stage (Eagle)** – The part of the Apollo 11 Lunar Module that remained on the Moon after the astronauts departed.
2. **Surveyor 3** – A robotic lunar lander visited by the Apollo 12 astronauts in 1969.
3. **Apollo 15 Lunar Roving Vehicle** – The first car driven by astronauts on the Moon.
4. **Viking 1 Lander** – One of the first successful spacecraft to land and operate on Mars.
5. **Opportunity Rover** – A Mars rover that operated for almost 15 years and helped scientists learn about the planet's watery past.

For each object, visitors can explore:

- **Mission:** The story behind its mission.
- **Discovery:** Important discoveries and achievements.
- **Where Is It Now?:** What happened to the equipment and where it remains.
- **Timeline:** Important events in its history.

The website also includes an interactive quiz to test what visitors have learned.

## How to Use the Website

1. Open the [live website](https://mah-sar.github.io/abandoned-but-not-forgotten-NasaSpaceApps/).
2. Start on the Home page to learn about the project.
3. Visit the Explore page to discover the five objects.
4. Select an object to read its story and explore its timeline.
5. Visit the Quiz page to test your knowledge.

## Development Process

We began by researching five pieces of space equipment left behind on the Moon and Mars.

After choosing the objects, we planned the website around three main pages: Home, Explore, and Quiz.

We organised the project into separate files to keep the content, design, and functionality manageable:

- `index.html` – Home page.
- `explore.html` – Explore page and object stories.
- `quiz.html` – Interactive quiz.
- `style.css` – Website styling and layout.
- `data.js` – Information, stories, timelines, and image sources for the objects.
- `script.js` – JavaScript functionality and interactive features.
- `assets/` – Images and other project assets.

We used Claude as an AI-assisted development tool to help us understand and work with code, generate and improve parts of the website, troubleshoot problems, and explore design ideas. Our team made decisions about the project's concept, research, content, design, and features, and reviewed and adapted the generated code.
