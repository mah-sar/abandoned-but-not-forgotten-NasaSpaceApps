# Abandoned but Not Forgotten 🚀

**A NASA Space Apps 2026 project by Team NEBIX**

This website is about five pieces of space equipment that were left behind on the Moon and Mars. Even though their missions have ended, they are still part of space exploration history.

## What you can explore

The website covers five objects:

- Apollo 11 Lunar Module Descent Stage
- Surveyor 3
- Apollo 15 Lunar Roving Vehicle
- Viking 1 Lander
- Opportunity Rover

For each one, you can learn about its mission, what it discovered, and where it is now.

There is also a timeline to explore and a quiz to test what you have learned.

## How to use the website

1. Open `index.html` to start on the home page.
2. Go to **Explore** to learn about the five objects.
3. Visit **Quiz** to test your knowledge.

You can also visit the live website here:

[Open the NEBIX website](https://mah-sar.github.io/abandoned-but-not-forgotten-NasaSpaceApps/)

## About the project

We made this website for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. Our goal is to make the stories of these forgotten pieces of space equipment easier and more interesting to explore.
